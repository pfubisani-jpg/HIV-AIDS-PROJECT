Why HIV Treatment Adherence Matters

Taking HIV treatment as prescribed helps control the virus, protect the immune system and maintain good health.

Benefits of HIV Treatment Adherence

Consistent use of antiretroviral therapy (ART) helps people living with HIV stay healthy and achieve viral suppression.

01

Strengthens the Immune System

Effective ART allows the immune system to recover and helps the body fight infections and other illnesses.

Learn more →
02

Keeps Viral Load Low

Consistent ART can stop HIV from multiplying and reduce the amount of virus in the blood to an undetectable level.

Understand viral load →
03

Prevents HIV Progression

Consistent treatment helps prevent HIV from progressing to advanced HIV disease and reduces HIV-related illness and death.

Learn more →
04

U = U

Undetectable = Untransmittable

A person living with HIV who is taking ART and maintains an undetectable viral load has zero risk of sexually transmitting HIV to their sexual partners.

Explore U = U →
05

Helps Prevent Drug Resistance

Poor adherence can allow HIV to multiply while exposed to medication, increasing the risk of developing drug resistance.

Learn about resistance →
06

Improves Quality of Life

Effective HIV treatment enables people living with HIV to live healthy, productive lives and maintain their daily activities.

Learn more →
07

Helps Protect Babies

Appropriate ART during pregnancy and breastfeeding is an important part of preventing HIV transmission to the baby.

Learn more →
BARRIERS & STRATEGIES

What Can Make Adherence Difficult?

Different people experience different barriers to staying on HIV treatment. Understanding the barrier is the first step toward finding a solution.

Forgetting Medication

A busy schedule or changes in routine can make it difficult to remember daily medication.

Strategy

Use phone alarms, SMS reminders, medication reminder apps or a pillbox.

Too Many Pills

Complex medication routines can make treatment difficult to maintain.

Strategy

Simplified treatment routines and combination medicines may reduce the daily pill burden.

Lack of Information

Not understanding how treatment works can affect motivation and adherence.

Strategy

Use educational videos, animations and personalised information.

Treatment Fatigue & Beliefs

Some people may become tired of treatment or question whether medication is still necessary when they feel healthy.

Strategy

Counselling, motivational interviewing and cognitive-behavioural approaches can provide support.

Financial & Practical Barriers

Transport costs, appointments and difficulty accessing medication can interfere with treatment.

Strategy

Practical support such as transport assistance, flexible services and medication access can help.

Protect Your Privacy

Medication reminders should never reveal someone's HIV status.

Instead of: "Take your HIV medication"

Use: "Medication reminder"

ALTERNATIVE OPTION

Long-Acting Injectable ART

Long-acting injectable ART, such as cabotegravir plus rilpivirine (CAB/RPV), can reduce the need for daily oral medication for eligible patients.

Why consider it?

Injectable treatment may help people who find it difficult to remember daily tablets.

What it does not solve

Injectable ART does not remove the need for clinic appointments and does not automatically solve transport problems, stigma, treatment concerns or medication access barriers.
